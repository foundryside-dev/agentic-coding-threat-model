Supporting material including the autonomy self-assessment framework, SQL extension case study, case studies with annotated agent transcripts, cross-model defect analysis, systems thinking primer, and glossary.

| Appendix | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| [Autonomy Self-Assessment]({{< relref "autonomy-assessment" >}}) | Informal diagnostic for agent deployment level and proportionate controls — four-level spectrum from full human to autonomous, with worked example of mixed-level deployment and evidence requirements for assessors |
| [SQL Extension Case Study]({{< relref "sql-extension" >}}) | ACF taxonomy applied to SQL — COALESCE as fabricated default, INSERT...SELECT crossing authority tiers, audit trail destruction via silent overwrites, partial completion without transactions, plus SQL-specific risks (implicit type coercion, dynamic SQL privilege escalation) and the citizen programmer intersection |
| [Case Studies]({{< relref "case-study" >}}) | Two evidence bases: a controlled simulation producing 20 semantic defects in a government assistance application (3 Critical, 16 undetectable by standard tools), and six months of longitudinal observation on a compliance-constrained project with annotated transcripts of three incidents spanning code-level, design-level, and specification-level failure surfaces |
| [Cross-Model Defect Chaining]({{< relref "cross-model-defects" >}}) | Second-order systemic risk from multi-model environments — how distinct defect tendencies from different models may compose rather than cancel, lineage concentration in the model ecosystem, and why "different agencies use different models" is not a sufficient independence argument |
| [Systems Thinking Primer]({{< relref "systems-thinking" >}}) | Feedback loops (reinforcing and balancing), the "Shifting the Burden" archetype, stock-flow dynamics (review backlog and latent defect accumulation), and Meadows' leverage points mapped to the controls this paper proposes — from parameters (low leverage) through paradigm shift (high leverage) |
| [Glossary]({{< relref "glossary" >}}) | Full definitions for all terms used throughout the threat model, including authority tiers, trust boundaries, control law states, ACF taxonomy entries, companion specification terms, and systems thinking concepts |
